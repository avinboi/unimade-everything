# Unimade business glossary

*Every business term used for Unimade, in plain words, with a Unimade example. Written 24 Nov 2026. Examples use CP3 numbers unless noted; CP3 is the submitted plan, and some numbers (Pass, kit) changed after it, see `UNIMADE_WHAT_WE_DO.md`.*

## Money in
| Term | Meaning | Unimade example |
|---|---|---|
| **D2C** (direct-to-consumer) | Selling directly to customers, with no retailer in between | Students book from Unimade itself |
| **GMV** (gross merchandise value) | Total money students pay, before anyone takes a cut | ₹3.97 lakh in CP3's 90 days |
| **Revenue / "keep"** | The part of GMV that's actually yours | ~₹21 of every ₹69/kg |
| **Spread** | Price to student minus payout to partner | ₹69 − ₹45 = ₹24/kg |
| **Commission / take rate** | The % you take from each sale | ~28% blended |
| **Subscription** | Paying a fixed amount every month | Pass ₹799, Pass Plus ₹1,500 |
| **Breakage** | Credits paid for but never used, which become extra profit | Assume 85% of pass credits get used |
| **Affiliate commission** | The % a brand pays you per sale made with your code | 5–15% on food-brand codes |
| **MRP vs selling price** | Printed maximum price vs the price you actually charge | — |

## Costs and profit
| Term | Meaning | Unimade example |
|---|---|---|
| **COGS** (cost of goods sold) | Direct cost of delivering one sale | Partner ₹45/kg + ₹2 other |
| **Gross margin** | (Price − COGS) ÷ Price | ₹69 − ₹48.38 = ₹20.62 kept → 29.9% on wash & fold |
| **Blended margin** | Average margin across everything you sell | ~28% (26.1% in CP3, before the Pass was dropped) |
| **Contribution margin** | Gross profit minus marketing and other per-order costs. Not formally defined in the course, so define it when you use it | First 4 kg order: ₹82.48 gross − ₹83 CAC ≈ ₹0. Repeat orders ≈ ₹82 each |
| **Fixed costs** | Costs that don't change with sales | ₹1,500/month for tools |
| **Payment gateway fee** | What UPI/card processing costs | 2% |
| **GST / TCS** | Tax on fees / 1% tax marketplaces hold back (claimable later) | S16 marketplace example |
| **Net payout** | What reaches your bank after marketplace fees | — |
| **Unit economics** | Profit or loss on one customer or one order | — |
| **ROI** (return on investment) | (Return − cost) ÷ cost | 2.5× in CP3 |
| **65% gate** | The course's (S16) minimum gross margin for quick commerce | Services fail at ~28% |

## Marketing
| Term | Meaning | Unimade example |
|---|---|---|
| **ROAS** (return on ad spend) | Revenue from ads ÷ ad spend | — |
| **Break-even ROAS** | 1 ÷ gross margin: the minimum ROAS to not lose money | 1 ÷ 0.28 = 3.57× |
| **MER** (marketing efficiency ratio) | Total revenue ÷ total marketing spend | 13.2 in CP3 |
| **CAC** (customer acquisition cost) | Marketing spend ÷ new customers | ₹83 in CP3 |
| **CPC** (cost per click) | What one ad click costs | Meta ₹7, Google ₹18 (CP3 estimates) |
| **CTR** (click-through rate) | Clicks ÷ people who saw the ad | — |
| **CVR / conversion rate** | Buyers ÷ clicks | Meta 4.2%, Google 6% |
| **CPM** | Cost per 1,000 ad views | — |
| **Quality Score** | Google's rating of how relevant your ad and landing page are | — |
| **TOFU / MOFU / BOFU** | Funnel stages: top (awareness) → middle (consideration) → bottom (purchase) | CP4 needs this mapping |
| **Paid / owned / earned / conversational** | Ads you pay for / your own channels / free buzz / WhatsApp and email | CP3 budget buckets |
| **ATL / BTL** | Mass offline ads / targeted offline (events, flyers) | Posters, "Weigh Your Week" pop-up |
| **60/30/10** | Offline budget split: safe bet / test / wild card | ₹3,600 / ₹1,800 / ₹600 |
| **Cold / warm / hot audience** | Strangers / people who engaged / site visitors | Meta ₹1,400 cold, ₹600 retarget |
| **Retargeting** | Showing ads again to people who already engaged | — |
| **UTM** | Tags on links that show where traffic came from | `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=cpc` |
| **Pixel** | Meta's tracking code that records actions on your site | ViewContent, Contact, Purchase |
| **Attribution** | Deciding which channel gets credit for a sale. The course doesn't teach a model, so pick one and say so | CP4 needs this |
| **North star metric** | The one number that shows the business is working | Repeat pickups per week |

## Customers and retention
| Term | Meaning | Unimade example |
|---|---|---|
| **LTV** (lifetime value) | Order value × orders a year × years × margin | ₹310 × 10 × 1 × 26% = ₹809 in CP3 |
| **LTV:CAC** | Customer value ÷ cost to win them. Below 1 loses money, ~3 is healthy, above 5 means under-spending on growth | 9.7:1 in CP3 |
| **Repeat rate** | Returning customers ÷ all customers | 35% in the retention snapshot |
| **Retention / churn** | % of customers who stay / % who leave | 55% reorder after a month |
| **Cohort** | Customers grouped by the month they joined | — |
| **Attach rate** | % of customers who also buy a second thing | Laundry users adding tiffin |
| **AOV** (average order value) | Revenue ÷ number of orders | ₹310 |
| **ARPU** (average revenue per user) | Revenue ÷ users | ₹58/user (v2 plan) |
| **Leakage** | Customers going direct to the vendor and skipping you | Biggest risk for a marketplace |
| **Payback period** | How long until a customer has paid back their CAC | — |
