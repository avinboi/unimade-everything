CHATGPT SETUP · STEP 1 OF 3
Teaching ChatGPT Your Brand Voice
Custom Instructions tell ChatGPT who you're writing for, every single time — so you stop re-explaining your brand in every prompt.
1 Open Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions
2 In "What would you like ChatGPT to know about you?", paste your brand facts: category, audience, price point, 2–3 real product details
3 In "How would you like ChatGPT to respond?", describe tone: 3 adjectives, one thing to avoid, preferred length
4 Save. Now every new chat starts already knowing your brand — no re-briefing needed

CHATGPT SETUP · STEP 2 OF 3
Creating a Project for Ongoing Content Work
A Project is a dedicated workspace — its own memory, files, and instructions — so your brand's content work doesn't get mixed up with everything else.
11 Click "Projects" in the sidebar → "New Project" → name it (e.g. "Breww 47 Content")
22 Add project-level instructions: brand voice, audience, and formatting rules — these apply to every chat inside the project automatically
33 Upload reference files: brand guidelines, past best-performing captions, a product list — ChatGPT reads these before responding
44 Every new chat you start inside the project inherits all of this — no repeating context, ever

CHATGPT SETUP · STEP 3 OF 3
Saving Reusable Outputs with Canvas
Canvas is ChatGPT's editable workspace for a piece of output — use it to save and refine things you'll reuse, instead of losing them in chat scroll.
1 When ChatGPT generates something worth keeping, ask it to "put this in canvas"
2 Canvas opens the content in a separate editable panel — tweak it directly without re-prompting
3 Save it: copy into your prompt bank doc (Notion, Google Doc, or spreadsheet) so it survives outside the chat
4 Rule of thumb: if you'll reuse it more than twice, it belongs in your prompt bank — not just in chat history

OBJECTIVE 3 · INFLUENCER STRATEGY
What Kind of Influencer Fits Your Category?
Before you search, decide who you're even looking for — the right creator TYPE depends on category and objective, not just budget.
F&B / Cafes (Breww 47) Hyperlocal micro-creators (2K–20K) + food/lifestyle reviewers in-city Search by city + #cafehopping / #foodblogger; geotag the outlet
Beauty & Personal Care Mid-tier creators (20K–150K) with strong tutorial/GRWM content Discovery tools filtered by category + engagement rate + past collabs
Fashion & Apparel Style-led creators + a small set of macro/celeb for launch buzz Look-book style match, not just follower count; check sentiment
D2C Tech / Appliances Niche 'how it works' reviewers and unboxing creators YouTube + Reels reviewers; check watch-through, not just likes
B2B / Services Founders, operators and category experts — not lifestyle creators LinkedIn search by role/industry (next slide), not Instagram

OBJECTIVE 3 · INFLUENCER STRATEGY
What Kind of Arrangement Do You Want?
1 Commission / Affiliate
Pay a % of sales via a tracked code or link. Best for: performance-focused, 
lower upfront risk, a wide funnel of small creators.
2 Awareness / Reach Deal
Flat fee for a post/story/reel to a specific audience. Best for: launches, reach 
into a new city or segment.
3 Brand Fan / Ambassador
Ongoing relationship, often gifting plus a small retainer. Best for: long-term 
trust-building, real product love.
4 Gifting / Barter
Free product in exchange for a post, no cash. Best for: small brands, new 
products, testing a relationship first.

OBJECTIVE 3 · INFLUENCER STRATEGY
LinkedIn: The Influencer Platform You're Ignoring
Instagram isn't the only place creators/experts have influence. LinkedIn works differently — and for some objectives, works better.
Instagram-fit brands & goals
•   Visual, emotional, lifestyle-driven purchases
•   Impulse or low-consideration buys
•   Objective: reach, discovery, awareness at volume
•   Audience decides fast, scrolls fast
LinkedIn-fit brands & goals
•   B2B brands — decision-makers ARE the audience here
•   High-consideration B2C (finance, health, real estate)
•   Objective: credibility, trust, thought leadership, hiring
•   Founder-led content and category expertise travel far

OBJECTIVE 3 · TOOL WALKTHROUGH
Finding the Right Influencers with Modash
Modash is an AI-powered discovery tool — search millions of creators and filter by audience fit and engagement, not just follower count.
1 Search by category, location and follower range Set your category-fit type from two slides ago, and a realistic follower band
2 Filter by engagement rate, not follower count A 10K creator at 6% often outperforms a 100K creator at 0.8%
3 Check audience overlap Confirm audience location/age/gender actually matches YOUR customer
4 Review past brand collaborations See what other brands a creator has worked with, and how it performed
5 Export your shortlist 8–10 creators with contact details — feeds into Session 11's outreach
