10 – 20 MIN
Anatomy of a High-Converting Bio
What You Sell
Stated plainly, no jargon. A stranger 
should know your category in one glance.
Who It's For
The specific customer, not everyone. 
Naming your buyer builds instant 
relevance.
Why Trust You
A credibility signal — a number, a claim, 
a guarantee, or a founder story.

--- page 6 ---
FRAMEWORK
The 3 Second Bio Rule
What you sell + who it's for + why trust you — readable in 3 seconds.
THE TEST
If it takes longer than 3 seconds to parse, it's too clever or too vague — rewrite it, don't defend it.

WHY IT MATTERS
Why a Link-in-Bio Tool?
THE PROBLEM
Instagram gives you exactly ONE 
clickable link in your bio.
But you probably want to send people to your website, your 
latest offer, WhatsApp, and your outlet locator — all at once.
THE FIX
A link-in-bio tool turns that 1 link into a 
mini landing page with multiple buttons.
One link in your bio → one page → as many destinations as 
you need, each with its own button.

WHY TRUST IT “[X] outlets across the city” — a real number signals this is an established, running business.
