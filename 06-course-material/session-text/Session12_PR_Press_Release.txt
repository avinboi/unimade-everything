0 – 10 MIN
How Startups Actually Get Covered by Media
WHAT JOURNALISTS IGNORE
“We're excited to announce...” — excitement is not 
news. A journalist gets dozens of these a day.
WHAT JOURNALISTS COVER
A specific, surprising fact their reader would want to 
know — with a name and a number attached.

--- page 5 ---
10 – 25 MIN  ·  FRAMEWORK
The So What Test
A hook only survives if it answers ‘so what’ twice in a row.
CLAIM “We launched a new flavor.”
SO WHAT? “It's the first zero-sugar cold brew made with local Indian beans.”
SO WHAT? “It lets a diabetic customer order a cold brew for the first time — a segment the category has ignored.”

35 – 45 MIN
Pitching Journalists Directly vs Using a PR Agency
Direct Pitch
Free, fast, and you control the story — but you need a real 
relationship or a genuinely strong angle to get a reply.
PR Agency
Costs money and takes longer to ramp up, but brings existing 
journalist relationships and pitch experience.

WORKED EXAMPLE  ·  A REAL BUSINESS
Breww 47's Mock Press Release
HEADLINE
Delhi-based coffee outlet Breww 47 expands to 3 new office parks after 71% of daily orders land in a single 
5-hour window
DATELINE
New Delhi, [Month] 2026
LEAD
Breww 47, a D2C coffee outlet brand, today announced expansion into 3 new office parks, after internal data showed 71–74% of daily orders consistently 
landing between 11am and 4pm — a pattern the founder is calling the city's ‘real lunch rush.’
BOILERPLATE
Breww 47 is a D2C coffee outlet brand built around speed during the workday rush.

ASK YOURSELF FIRST
Would this actually interest a stranger?
1 Is there genuinely a newsworthy angle here, or would this pitch bore a journalist?
2 What kind of story does your category naturally generate — a founder story, a data story, or a trend story?
3 If a journalist only read your headline, would they still understand why it matters?
