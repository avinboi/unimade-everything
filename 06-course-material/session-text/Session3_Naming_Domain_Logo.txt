0 – 10 MIN
What's in a Name?
NAMES THAT STICK
• Short and easy to say out loud
• Hints at the category or the feeling
• Survives being said over a noisy call
NAMES THAT FADE
• Hard to spell after hearing it once
• Generic — could belong to any company
• Tries to say everything at once

10 – 20 MIN
3 Naming Techniques
Descriptive Names the function directly — builds instant clarity and trust. Minimalist, The Whole Truth, SuperBottoms
Invented A made-up word — builds a distinctive, ownable identity. Zingavita, Antinorm, 82°E
Metaphor Borrows meaning from another concept — builds feeling and lifestyle association. Rage Coffee, Bewakoof, Snitch
DISCUSS: Does your category reward descriptive naming (protein, skincare) or evocative naming (apparel, coffee)?

FRAMEWORK
Say It, Spell It, Search It
A name only passes if it clears all three tests.
Say It
Can someone hear it once and say it back 
correctly?
Spell It
Can someone type it correctly after only 
hearing it?
Search It
Is the domain and handle actually 
available?

WORKED EXAMPLE  ·  A REAL BUSINESS
The Framework in Action: Breww 47
SAY IT Two easy syllables — reads clean out loud.
SPELL IT The double-w is the one trade-off: memorable, but occasionally gets typed as one W.
SEARCH IT The unusual spelling plus the number makes the name distinctive enough to secure.
TECHNIQUE BLEND: descriptive root (“Brew” = coffee) + invented spelling (the extra w) + a number for intrigue. Real brand nam es often blend techniques 
rather than pick one purely.

45 – 55 MIN
Logo Principles for Non-Designers
Simplicity
Must work as a small icon, not just a big 
banner. If it's confusing at thumbnail size, 
simplify it.
Scalability
Legible at favicon size and at billboard 
size — test both before you commit.
1–2 Colors Max
Don't design a rainbow. Fewer colors 
read as more premium and more 
consistent.

ASK YOURSELF FIRST
Before you lock this name in
1 Does your category reward a descriptive name that builds trust fast, or an evocative name that signals feeling?
2 Will this name still make sense if you expand into adjacent products in 2 years?
3 If you had to say this name over a bad phone line, would the other person get it right?
