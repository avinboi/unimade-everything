=== PAGE ===
20–26 MIN
UTM: A Label on Every Link
breww47.com/utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=cold-brew-launch
SOURCE
Where the click came from
instagram, google, whatsapp
MEDIUM
What kind of traffic it is
cpc for paid, social for organic posts
CAMPAIGN
Which push it belongs to
cold-brew-launch
THE RULE
All lowercase. No spaces. Same names every time. Instagram and instagram count as two different sources.

else on the team will type the same way. The next link says fb, and the 
data splits.

38–44 MIN
Meta Pixel: Count What People Do
A small piece of code on your site that tells Meta when someone takes an action, so you can see what your ads achieved.
STANDARD EVENTS: THE ACTIONS META ALREADY HAS NAMES FOR
ViewContent
Someone views a key page
AddToCart
An item goes in the cart
InitiateCheckout
Checkout starts
Purchase
A purchase is completed
Lead
A form or enquiry is submitted
Contact
Someone calls, texts or chats
Test it before you trust it. Browsers can block some tracking, so important events are also sent from the server.
