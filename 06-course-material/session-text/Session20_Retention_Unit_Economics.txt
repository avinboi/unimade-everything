12–20 MIN  ·  TEACH
Repeat Rate vs New Customer Rate
ONE MONTH:  200 customers ordered. 140 were buying for the first time. 60 had ordered before.
NEW CUSTOMER RATE
First time customers ÷ all customers
140 ÷ 200 =  70%
REPEAT RATE
Returning customers ÷ all customers
60 ÷ 200 =  30%
A high new rate with a low repeat rate means you are paying for every sale all over again.

35–41 MIN  ·  TEACH
The CAC to L TV Math
CAC
What it costs to win a customer
Marketing spend ÷ new customers
₹50,000 ÷ 100 = ₹500
L TV
What a customer is worth over time
Order value × orders per year × years
Then × gross margin for profit
THE RATIO
Is the customer worth the cost?
LTV ÷ CAC
₹1,500 ÷ ₹500 = 3 to 1
WHAT THE RATIO TELLS YOU
Below 1 to 1
You lose money on every customer.
Around 3 to 1
The widely quoted healthy level.
Above 5 to 1
Often means you are under investing in growth.
A rule of thumb, not a law. Count L TV on profit, not revenue.

60–65 MIN  ·  DEBATE
Repeat Driven or Acquisition Driven?
YOUR TURN
REPEAT DRIVEN
People need it again and again.
Coffee, skincare, supplements, pet food
Put the next rupee on bringing customers back.
ACQUISITION DRIVEN
People buy it once in a while.
Gifting, furniture, wedding items, big gadgets
Put the next rupee on finding new customers.
Subscription: they agree to come back in advance. One time purchase: you win them again each time.
Move to the side that fits your category. One minute to prepare, then each side defends its priority.
