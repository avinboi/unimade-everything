20 – 30 MIN
Newsletter Tool Setup: Brevo vs Substack
Brevo
Full email marketing tool — automations, branded templates, 
list segmentation. Best if you want your newsletter to look and 
feel like your website.
Substack
Fastest to start, built for writing — has its own built-in audience 
discovery. Best if the writing itself is the product.

--- page 8 ---
WORKED EXAMPLE  ·  A REAL BUSINESS
Welcome in 3: Breww 47's Sequence
1
THANKS
“Welcome to the BeanBelt —here's what's brewing”
Confirms the signup, sets expectations: weekly brew tips + first access to new blends.
2
STORY
“Why we started brewing at 47”
The founder story — the peak-hour insight behind Breww 47, told in a couple of honest paragraphs.
3
OFFER
“Your first cup is on us”
A concrete reason to visit an outlet this week — a first-order discount or a free add-on.

--- page 11 ---
ASK YOURSELF FIRST
Is email even the right channel for your buyer?
1 Is email a channel your specific buyer uses at your price point and age group, or would a WhatsApp broadcast get a stronger response?
2 Do you have enough of a story to justify a 3-email sequence yet, or just one honest email for now?
3 If you only sent one of these 3 emails, which one could your business not survive skipping?
