0 – 10 MIN
Consistency Beats Intensity
15 POSTS IN A BURST
Then silence for 3 weeks. The algorithm and the 
audience both forget you existed.
4 POSTS A WEEK, EVERY WEEK
Slower, but it compounds — a following builds only 
when it can predict you'll show up.

25 – 45 MIN
Frequency Planning & Theme Weeks
How Many Posts a Week Is Sustainable?
Pick a number you can hit for 4 straight weeks without burning out 
— 3 consistent beats 7 that stop after 10 days.
Theme Weeks vs Evergreen Slots
Some weeks can rally around one theme; other slots stay 
evergreen and reusable regardless of season.

45 – 80 MIN  ·  BUILD SPRINT
Build Your Full Month
Fill every cell of your 4x4 Grid — 4 buckets across 4 weeks.
• Use your Session 8 1-week map as the template for week 1.
• No empty cells — if you're stuck, that's a sign the bucket needs to change, not that the slot can stay blank.
Google Sheets or Notion, both free tiers, are enough — no paid tool needed for this build.

--- page 10 ---
ASK YOURSELF FIRST
Do you actually have enough to fill this?
1 Do you actually have enough real content ideas to fill 4 distinct buckets, or are you forcing structure onto very little?
2 Which bucket matters most for your category — education for something like protein or skincare, or entertainment for something like apparel?
3 If one bucket underperforms for a full month, would you have the data to know, or are you just guessing?
