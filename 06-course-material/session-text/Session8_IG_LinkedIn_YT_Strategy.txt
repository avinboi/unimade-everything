WORKED EXAMPLE  ·  A REAL BUSINESS
Framework: Peak Window Posting
Post when your audience is actually deciding — not just when you're free.
71–74% of Breww 47's daily orders land between 11am and 4pm — real order 
data, not a guess.
WHAT THIS CHANGES
Content and offers get pushed to match the 11am–4pm window, rather than posted whenever the founder happens to be free in the 
evening.

ASK YOURSELF FIRST
When does your buyer actually decide?
1 When does your specific customer actually make this buying decision — at a desk during work hours, late at night scrolling, or during a short break?
2 Is Hero content even useful for your category, or does your buyer mostly need Help content because the purchase is low involvement?
3 If you could only post once this week, which of your 7 slots could you not afford to cut?
