75 – 85 MIN  ·  PAIRS
Mobile Responsiveness Check
Open your site on your phone.

Swap phones with a partner — spot-check each other's sites for anything that breaks, overlaps, or gets cut off on mobile.
SCALING A D2C BUSINESS  ·  SESSION 6 Peer Check
--- page 11 ---
ASK YOURSELF FIRST
Does this site earn a stranger's next 3 seconds?
1 Does your business actually need a full multi-page site right now, or would a single sharp landing page do the job better?
2 What is the one thing above the fold that would make YOUR specific buyer stay — not a generic buyer?
3 If you removed the text, would the layout alone still signal what you sell?
