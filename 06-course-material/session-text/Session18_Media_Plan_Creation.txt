52–56 MIN
How to Measure Effectiveness?
ROAS
Return on Ad Spend
Revenue from ads ÷ ad spend
One channel or campaign. Did this ad pay for itself?
MER
Marketing Efficiency Ratio
Total revenue ÷ total marketing spend
The whole business. Is all our marketing paying off?
BREAK EVEN ROAS
1 ÷ gross margin. At 65% margin, 1 ÷ 0.65 = 1.54. Below that, each sale loses money before other costs.
SCALING A D2C BUSINESS  ·  SESSION 18 ROAS and MER
--- page 13 ---
56–59 MIN  ·  WORKED EXAMPLE
A Working Example
Illustrative numbers
ONE MONTH
Total revenue ₹4,00,000
Meta and Google ad spend ₹80,000
Revenue credited to those ads ₹2,00,000
Other marketing (creators, WhatsApp tool, 
posters) ₹70,000
Total marketing spend ₹1,50,000
ROAS
₹2,00,000 ÷ ₹80,000 = 2.5
MER
₹4,00,000 ÷ ₹1,50,000 = 2.67
BREAK EVEN ROAS
1 ÷ 0.65 = 1.54. The ads clear it.
MER counts sales the ads do not take credit for, such as repeat orders from WhatsApp.

64–66 MIN
Splitting the Budget Across Channels
1 Fix one total Decide the whole monthly budget first, in rupees.
2 Give each channel a job and a share Paid for new buyers, organic for trust, WhatsApp for repeat orders and follow ups.
3 Check the split with your numbers If a channel's ROAS beats break even, move more money to it. If not, cut it.
A starting point, not a standard: most money on what you can already measure, a small share to test the rest.
