5–10 MIN  ·  KEY TERMS
ATL and BTL in Plain Words
ATL speaks to many people at once. BTL speaks to a chosen few and asks them to act.
ATL  ·  ABOVE THE LINE
Mass media aimed at a wide audience.
EXAMPLES Television, radio, newspapers, billboards
BEST AT Making many people know your name
COST AND COUNTING Big cheques, hard to count who responded
BTL  ·  BELOW THE LINE
Targeted activity aimed at specific people.
EXAMPLES Events, sampling, flyers, offers, direct messages
BEST AT Getting a chosen person to act
COST AND COUNTING Smaller cheques, easier to count
WHY ATL STILL MATTERS FOR A SMALL BRAND
People buy what they recognise. A familiar name makes every other channel work better. A small brand does it at small scale: a poster 
where your buyer walks every day, not a TV ad.

KICKOFF  ·  10–13 MIN
What Offline Marketing Looks Like for a Small Brand
WHAT BIG BRANDS BUY
TV, radio, newspapers and billboards: wide reach, big 
cheques. This is what ATL, above the line, originally 
means.
WHAT A SMALL BRAND CAN DO
Posters, local tie ups and small events: narrow reach, 
small cheques, and you can see who responded.
In this course, ATL means any offline marketing you can afford.

48–55 MIN  ·  FRAMEWORK
The 60/30/10 Budget Split
Put most of the money on your best guess, some on a second guess, and a little on something wild.
60%
SAFE BET
The idea you have the most reason to trust ₹6,000
30%
TEST
A second idea you want to try, with a smaller bet ₹3,000
10%
WILD CARD
A small experiment. If it fails, you barely notice ₹1,000
Example on a ₹10,000 budget. A smaller or bigger budget uses the same split.
A rule of thumb, not an industry standard.
