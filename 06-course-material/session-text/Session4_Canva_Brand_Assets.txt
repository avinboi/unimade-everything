0 – 10 MIN
Why Inconsistent Visuals Kill Brand Recall
A SCROLLING FEED
A follower sees your post for less than a second 
before scrolling past. There's no time to read — only 
to recognize.
WHAT CONSISTENCY BUYS YOU
A recognizable color and layout means a scroller 
registers “I've seen this brand before” — before 
they've even read a word.

FRAMEWORK
Brand Kit Lock
Colors, fonts, and logo lockup are fixed before a single piece of content is made.
THE RULE
No new colors, no new fonts mid-project. Consistency requires discipline, not talent — and it's the cheapest 
professionalism signal you have.

45 – 75 MIN  ·  BUILD SPRINT
Batch Creation Sprint
Produce 3 posts and 1 story — using ONLY your locked Brand Kit.
• No new colors. No new fonts. That's the whole exercise.
• Mix formats — don't make 4 single images.
Justify your color choice against your actual target customer — not what looks nice to you personally.

--- page 11 ---
75 – 85 MIN  ·  PAIRS
Peer Critique Round
Show your 4 assets to a classmate. One question:
“Does this look like it belongs to one consistent brand —or four different ones?”
If the answer is 'four different ones,' find the inconsistency together before moving on.
SCALING A D2C BUSINESS  ·  SESSION 4 Peer Critique
--- page 12 ---
ASK YOURSELF FIRST
Whose eye are you actually designing for?
1 What does premium actually look like for your specific category — minimal and quiet, or bold and loud?
2 Would your actual target customer stop scrolling for this visual style, or are you copying what looks nice to you?
3 If you removed your logo, would a follower still recognize this as your brand from the colors alone?
